FridgeLight: the worst app idea ever pitched. One sentence, 237 characters,
pitching an app that pings your phone every time someone opens your fridge,
logs exactly how long the door stayed open, and ranks your household by
midnight-snack discipline — because your leftovers deserve 24/7 surveillance.

Built for make.fun's "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched" bounty ($2 in USDC,
top 8 paid). The judge compares pitches pairwise on specificity, humour and
how perfectly pointless the app is; FridgeLight aims squarely at the paid
places with a product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a
function nobody on earth needs.

## The pitch

> FridgeLight is the app that pings your phone every time someone opens your fridge, logs exactly how long the door stayed open, and ranks your whole household by midnight-snack discipline, because your leftovers deserve 24/7 surveillance.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the one-sentence pitch to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
